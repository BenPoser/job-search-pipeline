# Voice Profile template

This is the shape of `my/voice.md`. `/setup` writes the real one by reading writing samples the
user supplies, then shows the result to the user so they can correct it.

Everything here is personal taste. Craft standards that apply to everyone live in
`docs/quality-rules.md`, and they win where the two conflict.

The three sections exist separately because the same person writes differently across them. A
CV bullet, a cover letter and a competency answer are three different registers.

## CV bullets

The register here varies more than people expect. Get it wrong and the CV reads as written by
someone else, even when every fact is right.

- Structure: implied subject ("Redesigned the intake process") / first person / prose
- Does the metric lead or trail? ("Cut handling time by 40% by redesigning..." vs
  "Redesigned the intake process, cutting handling time by 40%")
